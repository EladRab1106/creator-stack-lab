Submagic sells three self-serve plans. Each video costs 3 credits, so the credit numbers translate directly into videos per month.

Pick by your weekly output

  • Up to ~3 Shorts a week: Starter covers it (15/month).
  • About one a day: Pro (40/month). It's also where silence removal and auto-zooms start, which is most of the editing time on talking-head clips.
  • Several a day or long podcasts: Business, mainly for the 30-minute limit and 100 videos.

Monthly or yearly?

Yearly billing is roughly 40% cheaper on every plan, but you pay upfront. If you're not sure you'll keep posting for a year, start monthly for a month and switch once the habit sticks.

Is there a free option?

Submagic's pricing page says you can start free with no credit card. If you only need plain captions, the free script in my guide Auto-caption Shorts for free does that offline.
